# HAUS Onboarding · B-roll design

## Style
Calm, premium, editorial product film on a flat cream page. Slow opacity fades, accurate product UI, one quiet accent. Nothing bounces, floats, pulses, zooms or glows.

## Colors (lilac, peach, cream only. Never black.)
- Cream `#F8F5EF` page, `#FFFDF9` cards/panels
- Deep lilac `#4A3270` text · plum `#6A4C9C` badges, buttons, rules
- Lilac `#B98DF0` / `#D8C2EC` highlights, ripples
- Peach `#E0926A` italic accent word + eyebrow labels (`#C9805A` for small caps)

## Typography
- The Seasons Light (300) for headlines, italic for the accent word (peach)
- Visby CF 400/500/600 for UI and body; eyebrows 600, 0.34em tracking, uppercase

## Never
- Black or pure-white elements, grids, ghost words, HUD bars, light streaks, confetti, orbits
- Floating/bobbing cards, sheens, pulsing, camera zoom or push-in, scale-in on cards
- Slide/blur word reveals (they shimmer on thin serifs): reveals are opacity-only
